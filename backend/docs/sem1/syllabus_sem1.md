## MA-101: Mathematics-I

**Objective:** To provide a strong foundation in calculus, linear algebra, and complex numbers needed in engineering problems.

### Unit 1: Matrices & Linear Algebra
- Types of matrices, Rank, Echelon form, Normal form
- System of linear equations (Cramer's rule, Gauss elimination)
- Eigenvalues, Eigenvectors, Cayley-Hamilton theorem, Diagonalization

### Unit 2: Differential Calculus
- Rolle's theorem, Mean value theorems (Lagrange, Cauchy)
- Taylor's and Maclaurin's series
- Partial derivatives, Total derivative, Euler's theorem on homogeneous functions

### Unit 3: Integral Calculus
- Definite & Improper integrals
- Beta and Gamma functions
- Double and Triple integrals, Change of order of integration

### Unit 4: Fourier Series
- Euler's formulae, Dirichlet's conditions
- Fourier series for Even and Odd functions
- Half range sine and cosine series
- Parseval's Identity

### Unit 5: Differential Equations
- First Order ODEs (variable separable, exact, linear)
- Second Order Linear ODEs with constant coefficients
- Cauchy-Euler equations, Method of variation of parameters

## CH-101: Chemistry-I

**Objective:** Understanding molecular structure, chemical bonding, spectroscopy, and their engineering applications.

### Unit 1: Molecular Structure & Bonding
- Molecular Orbital Theory (MOT), Bond order, Magnetic character
- Crystal Field Theory (CFT) for octahedral complexes, CFSE
- VBT, Hybridization

### Unit 2: Spectroscopic Techniques
- UV-Visible spectroscopy: Electronic transitions, Chromophores, Auxochromes
- IR Spectroscopy: Vibrational modes for polyatomic molecules
- NMR Spectroscopy: Components and principle

### Unit 3: Electrochemistry
- Electrochemical cells, EMF measurement, Nernst equation
- Conductance, Kohlrausch's law
- Batteries, Fuel cells, Corrosion and prevention

### Unit 4: Engineering Materials
- Polymers: Classification, Polymerization reactions, Properties
- Lubricants: Classification, Mechanism of lubrication, Viscosity
- Cement, Refractories, Abrasives

### Unit 5: Water Technology
- Hardness of water, Degree of hardness, Estimation
- Softening methods (Lime-Soda, Zeolite, Ion Exchange)
- Desalination, Water quality standards for boiler feed

## CS-101: Programming Fundamentals in C

**Objective:** To introduce structured programming concepts using C language.

### Unit 1: Introduction to Programming & C
- Problem solving, Algorithm, Flowchart
- Structure of C program, Data types, Variables, Constants, Operators

### Unit 2: Control Structures
- Conditional statements (if-else, switch-case)
- Looping (for, while, do-while), Break, Continue

### Unit 3: Functions & Storage Classes
- Function definition, declaration, call, return
- Recursion, Storage classes (auto, register, static, extern)
- String handling, Character arrays (null terminator, declaration)

### Unit 4: Arrays & Pointers
- 1D and 2D arrays, Pointer arithmetic
- Arrays of pointers, Dynamic memory allocation (malloc, calloc, realloc, free)

### Unit 5: Structures, Unions & File Handling
- Structures, Unions, Typedef, Enum
- File I/O operations, Sequential and Random access
