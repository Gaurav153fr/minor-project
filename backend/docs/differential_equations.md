# Differential Equations

## Linear Differential Equations of First Order
A differential equation of the form dy/dx + Py = Q, where P and Q are constants or functions of x only, is called a linear differential equation of the first order.

## Integrating Factor
To solve the linear differential equation dy/dx + Py = Q, we multiply both sides by an Integrating Factor (I.F.).
The Integrating Factor is given by:
I.F. = e^(int P dx)

## Solution
Multiplying the equation by I.F. makes the left side an exact derivative:
d/dx (y * I.F.) = Q * I.F.

Integrating both sides with respect to x gives the general solution:
y * I.F. = int (Q * I.F.) dx + C
Where C is the constant of integration.
